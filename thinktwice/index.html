Privacy Policy for Think Twice

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Think Twice does not collect your personal information. The game runs entirely on your device and every puzzle works offline. Your progress is saved only on your phone. The one component that collects data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the game free.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android game Think Twice (package com.trickygame.thinktwice), referred to below as “the app”. The app is published by an independent developer, referred to as “we” or “us”.

Your game progress

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • which levels you have solved, your stars and best times, and how many hints you used;
  • your daily-puzzle results and streak, your achievements, and secrets you have found;
  • small “memories” some puzzles leave for later levels (for example, what you put in a fridge);
  • simple play-style counters, such as how often you tap or drag, used to personalise one level and a few playful messages;
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration, theme and reduced motion.

This data never leaves your device. We do not back it up to a server, and we have no server that could receive it. Resetting progress in Settings, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Device sensors and buttons

Some puzzles are solved with the phone itself. While you play those levels, the app reads the motion sensor (to notice turning, tilting, shaking or laying the phone down), the proximity sensor (to notice the top of the phone being covered), the volume buttons, and whether a charger is connected. These readings are used instantly on your device to move the puzzle along. They are never stored, and never sent anywhere. None of them needs a permission.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a small banner, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for the last hint or the solution of a level. AdMob is operated by Google, not by us, and it is the only component in the app that collects information about you.

To serve ads and to measure and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, general device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive personal information through AdMob, and we cannot identify you from it.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are shown. You can change that choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob

You can also limit ad personalisation in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

We operate no servers and hold no database about you. We have no way to identify any individual player. If you share a daily-puzzle result, only the text you choose to share is sent, through the sharing app you pick.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet, network state and advertising ID: only for Google AdMob to load ads. The game itself never uses the network.

Vibration feedback uses the system's built-in haptics and needs no permission. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects nothing itself, the only relevant data handling is Google's, described above.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices (where shown).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Use Settings → Reset progress, clear the app's data, or uninstall it, to erase everything it saved.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
