Privacy Policy for TiltBound

Last updated: 5 October 2026

TiltBound does not collect your personal information. The game runs on your device and every level works offline. Your progress, settings and custom levels are saved only on your phone. The one component that collects data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the game free.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android game TiltBound (package com.example.tiltbound), referred to below as “the app”. The app is published by an independent developer, referred to as “we” or “us”.

Your game progress and settings

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • which levels you have unlocked, your stars and your best times;
  • levels you build in the level editor;
  • your settings: haptics, sound, music, tilt sensitivity, sensor smoothing, touch controls, reduced motion and the FPS counter;
  • if you use Recalibrate Device, the phone angle you chose as “level” (three numbers describing orientation, nothing else).

This data never leaves your device. We do not back it up to a server, and we have no server that could receive it. Resetting progress in Settings, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Motion sensor

You steer the ball by tilting your phone. While a level is being played, and while the Settings screen shows its live tilt preview, the app reads the motion sensor (rotation vector, or the accelerometer on devices without one). These readings are used instantly on your device to move the ball. They are never stored, except for the calibration angle described above, and never sent anywhere. The sensor needs no permission and is switched off when you pause, leave a level, or put the app in the background.

Importing and exporting levels

The level editor can export a level as a JSON file and import one. In both cases you choose the file through Android's own file picker, and the app reads or writes only that file. It has no access to your other files.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a banner on the level list, a full-screen ad after every few completed levels, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to reveal a hint. AdMob is operated by Google, not by us, and it is the only component in the app that collects information about you.

To serve ads and to measure and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, general device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive personal information through AdMob, and we cannot identify you from it.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob

You can limit ad personalisation in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet, network state and advertising ID: only for Google AdMob to load ads. The game itself never uses the network.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback when the ball hits walls, portals and hazards. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects nothing itself, the only relevant data handling is Google's, described above.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Use Settings → Reset Progress, clear the app's data, or uninstall it, to erase everything it saved.

Changes to this policy
