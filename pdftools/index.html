Privacy Policy for PDF Toolkit

Last updated: 21 September 2026

PDF Toolkit does not upload your documents. Every PDF operation — merging, splitting, editing pages, OCR, password protection and the rest — runs entirely on your device. Your files are never sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that could receive them.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android app PDF Toolkit (package com.shiva.pdftools), referred to below as “the app”. The app is published by an independent developer, referred to as “we” or “us”.

Your documents and files

When you choose a PDF or an image, the app reads it directly on your device through Android's own file picker. The app:

  • does not upload, transmit, or back up your documents anywhere;
  • does not read your storage on its own — it only ever sees the specific files you pick;
  • does not ask for storage permission, because it does not need one;
  • writes the files it produces into the app's own private folder on your device.

Files the app creates stay on your device until you delete them. Getting a file out of the app is always an explicit action you take: sharing it, or saving it somewhere you choose.

History

The app keeps a History list of the files it has produced, stored in a database on your device only. Each entry records the file's name, its location on your device, its size, its page count, which tool made it, and when. This never leaves your device, and clearing your History or uninstalling the app removes it.

Optical character recognition (OCR)

Text recognition runs on your device using a model bundled inside the app. The pages it reads and the text it produces are not sent anywhere.

Document scanner

The Scan feature uses Google's document scanner, which is provided by Google Play services rather than by the app itself. Scanning happens on your device, but the scanner's interface is downloaded from Google the first time you use it, so that one feature requires an internet connection on first use. The app states this on the Scan screen. Your scanned pages are processed on your device and handed back to the app.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google, not by us, and it is the only component in the app that collects information about you.

To serve ads and to measure and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, general device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive your documents or personal information through AdMob, and we cannot identify you from it.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob

You can limit ad personalisation at any time in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can also delete or reset your advertising ID.

Why the app requests internet access

The app declares the internet permission for two reasons only: to load ads, and to let Google Play services download the document scanner the first time you use it. No PDF work uses the network. Apart from those two things, the app functions with no connection at all.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects nothing itself, the only relevant data handling is Google's, described above.

Your choices

  • Delete any file the app made from the History tab, or clear the whole History.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes its files and its History from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
