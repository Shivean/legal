Privacy Policy for Arrow Drift

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Arrow Drift does not collect your personal information. The game runs entirely on your device and works offline. Your progress is saved only on your phone. The one component that collects data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the game free.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android game Arrow Drift (package com.arrowdrift.game), referred to below as “the app”. The app is published by an independent developer, referred to as “we” or “us”.

Your game progress

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • which levels you have unlocked, and your stars for each level, in Easy and Hard mode;
  • how many hints you have;
  • your settings: theme, difficulty, and whether vibration is on.

This data never leaves your device. We do not back it up to a server, and we have no server that could receive it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Every puzzle is generated on your device, so playing needs no internet connection.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a small banner, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for an extra heart or extra hints. AdMob is operated by Google, not by us, and it is the only component in the app that collects information about you.

To serve ads and to measure and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, general device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive personal information through AdMob, and we cannot identify you from it.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised. You can change that choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob

You can also limit ad personalisation in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads, and to hide the ad area while you are offline. The game itself never uses the network.
  • Vibration: for the short buzzes when an arrow flies off or gets blocked. You can turn this off in Settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects nothing itself, the only relevant data handling is Google's, described above.

Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices (where shown).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Clear the app's data, or uninstall it, to erase your saved progress.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
