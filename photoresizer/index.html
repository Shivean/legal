Privacy Policy for Photo Resizer

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Photo Resizer does not upload your photos. Every photo operation — passport and ID photos, resizing, compression, conversion, editing, blurring faces, removing metadata and making PDFs — runs entirely on your device. Your photos are never sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that could receive them.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android app Photo Resizer (package com.shiva.photoresizer), referred to below as “the app”. The app is published by an independent developer, referred to as “we” or “us”.

Your photos

When you choose a photo, the app reads it directly on your device through Android's own photo picker or file picker, or takes it with the in-app camera. The app:

  • does not upload, transmit, or back up your photos anywhere;
  • does not browse your gallery on its own — it only ever sees the specific photos you pick;
  • does not ask for storage or photo permissions, because it does not need them;
  • keeps working copies only in its own private folder on your device and deletes them automatically.

Getting a result out of the app is always an explicit action you take: saving it to your Pictures or Download folder, saving it somewhere you choose, sharing it, or printing it.

Face detection and background replacement

The passport photo and blur-faces tools find faces, and the passport tool can replace the background. Both use Google ML Kit models that are bundled inside the app and run on your device, even in airplane mode. Faces, face measurements and the photos themselves are not stored after you leave the screen and are never sent anywhere. The app does not recognise or identify people.

Camera

The camera permission is requested only when you open the in-app camera, and it is used only to take the photo you are about to edit. Captured photos stay on your device.

Photo metadata

Photos can contain hidden details such as GPS location, camera model and the date taken. By default, Photo Resizer removes these from the files it creates. You can choose to keep them for a particular export. The blur-faces tool always removes them.

Settings

Your preferences (theme, default quality, metadata removal) and a few counters used to pace ads are stored on your device only and are not backed up or transmitted.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google, not by us, and it is the only component in the app that collects information about you.

To serve ads and to measure and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, general device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Ads never have access to your photos, and we cannot identify you from AdMob.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy options.

We operate no servers, use no analytics SDK, and hold no database about you. We have no way to identify any individual user of the app. We may see anonymous crash and performance statistics that Google Play provides to all developers, according to your device settings.

Why the app requests internet access

The app declares the internet permission only to load ads and the ad consent form. No photo work uses the network, and every tool works with no connection at all.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects nothing itself, the only relevant data handling is Google's, described above.

Your choices

  • Keep or remove photo metadata for each export (removed by default).
  • Delete the app's temporary files at any time in Settings.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy options (where required).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from your device.

Passport and ID photos

Photo sizes and guidelines in the app are based on public sources and may change. The issuing authority always makes the final decision about whether a photo is accepted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
