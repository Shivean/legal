Privacy Policy for Voice Changer

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Voice Changer never uploads your voice. Recording, importing audio files, every voice effect, playback and saving happen entirely on your device. Your recordings are never sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that could receive them. The one component that collects data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the app free.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android app Voice Changer (package com.shivean.voicechanger), referred to below as “the app”. The app is published by an independent developer, referred to as “we” or “us”.

Your microphone and recordings

The app asks for microphone access the first time you tap the record button, so it can record your voice. You can refuse or revoke it at any time in Android Settings. The app:

  • records only while you are recording, with the recording screen open — never in the background;
  • applies all voice effects (pitch, robot, echo, reverb and the rest) with audio-processing code built into the app, running on your device's processor — no cloud voice service or online API is involved, and effects work in airplane mode;
  • does not upload, transmit, or back up your recordings anywhere;
  • does not give your audio to the advertising SDK or to anyone else.

You can also import an audio file from your device. The file is chosen with Android's own file picker, so the app only ever sees the one file you pick, and it needs no storage permission. The file is decoded on your device into a working copy in the app's private storage; it is never uploaded. Your original file is not modified.

The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so its private data is not copied to Google backups. Files you save to the shared Music folder can be seen by you in a file manager and by other apps you have allowed to read audio files.

When you tap Share, Android's share sheet opens and you choose where the recording goes. The app grants the chosen app temporary access to that one file only; what happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: a small banner at the bottom of the main and Saved screens, and occasionally a full-screen ad after you save a recording. AdMob is operated by Google, not by us, and it is the only component in the app that collects information about you. It never receives your recordings.

To serve ads and to measure and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, general device and app information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive personal information through AdMob, and we cannot identify you from it.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalised. You can change that choice at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services

You can also limit ad personalisation in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID.

Permissions the app uses

  • Microphone: to record your voice when you tap record.
  • Internet and network state: only to load ads, and to hide the ad area while you are offline. Recording and voice effects never use the network.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects nothing itself, the only relevant data handling is Google's, described above.

Your choices

  • Revoke microphone access at any time in Android Settings.
  • Discard recordings in the app; delete saved recordings in the Saved screen or a file manager.
  • Change your ad consent in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices (where shown).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Clear favorites and recents in Settings, or clear the app's data or uninstall it to erase everything it stored privately.
