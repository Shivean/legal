Privacy Policy for Echo Pulse

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Echo Pulse does not collect your personal information. The game runs entirely on your device, works offline, and has no ads, analytics, or accounts. It does not even have permission to use the internet.

Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android game Echo Pulse: Dark Sonar (package com.shivean.echopulse), referred to below as “the app”. The app is published by an independent developer, referred to as “we” or “us”.

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • your settings: volume levels, and whether haptic feedback and spatial audio are on;
  • your game progress, such as which levels you have completed, your best times and the fewest sonar pulses you used;
  • the game's sound effects, which the app generates itself the first time it runs.

None of this leaves your device. We do not back it up to a server, and we have no server that could receive it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

We operate no servers and hold no database about you. We have no way to identify any individual player.

Permissions the app uses

  • Vibration: for short haptic cues when you send a sonar pulse, bump into a wall, or complete a level. You can turn this off in Settings.

The Privacy policy link in Settings opens this page in your web browser. The app itself never connects to the internet.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the app collects nothing, there is no data about any player, of any age, for us to hold.

Your choices

  • Turn haptic feedback on or off in the app under Settings.
  • Clear the app's data, or uninstall it, to erase your settings and progress.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes affecting how data is handled will be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
